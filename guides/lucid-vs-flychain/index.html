Short answer

Lucid is the stronger choice for most medical and private practices. Lucid publishes its prices and a calculator, puts a dedicated CPA on every account, offers month to month terms, says most practices are live in about 24 hours, and has dedicated pages and reporting for dental, orthopedics, dermatology, primary care, med spa and medical groups. Flychain, a healthcare finance firm, does not list prices on its site, says onboarding takes two to three weeks, and adds financing products that Lucid does not offer.

Lucid

Lucid is bookkeeping, tax, tax credits and CFO services for medical and private practices, with a dedicated CPA on every account. It is built for practices with staff, several locations or several practices.

What Lucid gives you:

  1. A price you can see. Lucid publishes plans and an online calculator, with no setup fee and month to month terms. Flychain makes you book a demo first. Lucid starts from $130 a month. Bookkeeping and CFO are priced by your monthly expenses on the calculator, Tax Essentials is a flat monthly price, and practices with over $200,000 in monthly expenses get custom pricing.
  2. A faster start. Lucid says most practices are live in about 24 hours and scopes catch up work first, then gives a fixed price. Flychain says two to three weeks.
  3. One CPA led team. A dedicated CPA and account manager cover bookkeeping, tax and CFO planning, with a shared Slack channel and replies within one business day.
  4. Pages and reporting for your specialty. Lucid describes payer deposit reconciliation for primary care, margin per procedure for orthopedics, separate cosmetic, medical and retail revenue for dermatology, production and collections for dental, and membership revenue for med spas. Flychain has no dedicated specialty pages.
  5. Group reporting. Lucid reports each location and a combined view, with PC MSO structures and automated intercompany reconciliation.
  6. Tax credits at no risk. Lucid looks for R&D and hiring credits. If it finds none, you pay $0.

Good to know:

  1. Lucid does not offer financing.
  2. Lucid does not list payroll processing as a service.

Flychain

Flychain describes itself as "bookkeeping, CFO intelligence, taxes, and capital" for healthcare practices. Its site groups customers into new practices, established practices and multi location groups, MSOs and regional practices. Its guides say it serves medical, dental and behavioral health practices, and its named examples include ABA, home health and infusion therapy.

What Flychain gives you:

  1. Financing. It offers advances on claims and term loans, helps with SBA applications and introduces equity investors. Lucid has no financing product.
  2. Tax staffing it names. Returns are "prepared and filed by our team of CPAs and EAs," with a dedicated CFP for planning. About half of its clients use it for filing, and the rest keep their own CPA.
  3. An AI CFO tool. CFO Hub offers an AI CFO plus contracted rate analysis, specialty and salary benchmarking and practice valuation. Flychain lists cash flow forecasting and profitability by CPT code as coming soon.
  4. Connections. It connects to bank accounts, payroll and corporate cards with view only access, and asks about your EMR during onboarding. It says it works alongside tools such as Gusto, Stripe and Mercury.

What to weigh:

  1. No prices on its site, and a two to three week onboarding stated.
  2. Neither firm publishes a full integration list. Ask which systems connect to your books.

Who should choose which

  1. Choose Lucid if you run a medical practice, a group with several locations or an owner with several practices, and want a price you can see, a dedicated CPA and a fast start. See the pages for dental, orthopedics, dermatology, med spa, primary care and medical groups, or the medical practices overview.
  2. Choose Flychain if you want financing from the same firm as your books, or you run an ABA, home health or infusion practice and want a firm that names those models.
  3. Not sure? Compare the price. Lucid's is published. Flychain's comes after a demo.

Questions to ask each firm before you sign

Flychain and Lucid both serve healthcare practices, but they package the work differently. Put the same questions to both before you decide.

  1. What will it cost, and when do I see the number? Lucid shows prices on its calculator before any call. Flychain quotes after a demo or consult, so ask for the quote in writing.
  2. What are the terms? Lucid is month to month with no setup fee. Flychain's terms were not stated on the pages we reviewed, so ask about length, notice and setup charges.
  3. How long until my books are current? Lucid says most practices are live in about 24 hours, and catch up work is scoped first with a fixed price. Flychain says onboarding takes two to three weeks and scopes catch up work separately.
  4. Who prepares and reviews my tax return? Lucid says every account has a dedicated CPA. Flychain says returns are prepared and filed by its team of CPAs and EAs.
  5. Do I want financing from the same firm? Flychain offers advances on claims and term loans. Lucid focuses on bookkeeping, tax, tax credits and CFO planning, including a lender ready financial package.
  6. Which reports will I see monthly? Ask each firm for a sample package, including results by location or provider and revenue by payer.

If the answers point to a visible price, a fast start and a dedicated CPA, Lucid is the match. If financing from your accounting firm is the deciding factor, put Flychain on the shortlist. Either way, take notes on each answer and compare them side by side, because the firm that answers plainly before you sign tends to communicate plainly afterwards. Bring your last two months of bank statements and your current chart of accounts to each call, so the firms can scope your work from real numbers rather than guesses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Flychain or Lucid better for a medical practice?

Lucid is the stronger fit for most medical and private practices. It publishes its prices, gives every account a dedicated CPA, runs month to month, says most practices are live in about 24 hours, and has dedicated pages for dental, orthopedics, dermatology, primary care and med spa. Flychain is the pick mainly if you want financing from your accounting firm.

Does Flychain publish its prices?

Not on the pages we reviewed on November 26, 2026. Flychain quotes by practice size, locations and services, and sends visitors to book a demo or request a custom quote. Lucid publishes plans and pricing on its pricing page.

Who prepares my return at each firm?

Lucid says every account has a dedicated CPA. Flychain says returns are prepared and filed by its team of CPAs and EAs, with a dedicated CFP for planning. Ask each firm who prepares your return and who reviews it.

How fast can I switch?

Lucid says most practices are live in about 24 hours and scopes catch up work first for books that are behind. Flychain says two to three weeks. Neither defines "live" on its site, so ask what you get in week one.

Can either one help with financing?

Flychain can. It offers advances on claims and term loans, helps with SBA applications and introduces equity investors. Lucid does not offer financing. Lucid does look for R&D and hiring credits, and charges nothing if none are found.

Do both handle multiple locations?

Yes. Lucid reports each location and a combined view and covers PC MSO structures with intercompany reconciliation. Flychain names multi location groups and MSOs and compares locations on one chart of accounts.

Are both secure for patient related data?

Lucid says its security program is aligned to the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, offers HIPAA aligned handling and makes a Business Associate Agreement available. Flychain says its platform is "designed for HIPAA compliance." Ask each firm for current security documents before you sign.

What about reviews?

This guide does not rate either company on reviews. Check current, dated reviews on independent sites and ask each firm for references in your specialty.

Lucid fits medical and private practices with staff, several locations or several practices that want bookkeeping, tax and CFO support from one CPA led team, at a published price, on month to month terms. Book the free 30 minute consult at Lucid pricing or read about medical practices.
