Short answer

For most speech therapy groups, the shortlist is Fusion (Ensora), ClinicSource, TheraPlatform and SimplePractice, with WebPT and Raintree for larger rehab groups that also run PT and OT. Fusion and ClinicSource go deepest on insurance billing for SLPs, TheraPlatform leads on teletherapy tools, and SimplePractice is the easiest to set up. Before you buy, check how each one handles school and agency contracts, because most are built for claims and private pay first. None of these tools keeps your books, and Lucid is the accounting layer that sits beside whichever one you pick.

Fusion (Ensora)

Fusion is Ensora's practice management system for SLP, PT and OT practices, covering pediatric, adult and geriatric care. Ensora's pricing page lists Fusion from $25 a month for solo practices, or $159 or $229 a month for the first user in a multi clinician practice, plus $45 or $75 for each added user. Its billing pages describe electronic claims through the Ensora clearinghouse, with remits attached to claims for review. The higher Premier plan adds unlimited eligibility checks, claim submissions and telehealth. Pabau's 2026 review rates it best for pediatric speech therapy practices and notes SLP goal banks.

Best for: speech led pediatric groups that bill insurance and Medicaid. Limits: confirm which plan includes telehealth and how school contract sessions are invoiced.

ClinicSource

ClinicSource has served therapy practices since 2006, with notes, scheduling and billing for speech, occupational, physical and ABA therapy. Pabau's review lists a starting price of $74.95 a month and says tiers and user limits are not published. It highlights clearinghouse integration, ERA and EOB workflows and denial management built into billing.

Best for: groups with in house billing staff who work denials, and practices that mix SLP with OT or PT. Limits: hard to budget without a quote, because only the entry price is shown.

TheraPlatform

TheraPlatform combines practice management, notes, billing and teletherapy. Its pricing page lists Basic at $39 a month (one provider only), Pro at $69 plus $39 per added provider, and Pro Plus at $79 plus $49 per added provider, with no setup fee. Electronic claims are $0.25 per claim, eligibility checks are $0.10 per request, AI notes are $30 per provider and text reminders are $0.02 per message. Pabau notes that its interactive therapy apps start on Pro. TheraPlatform's own billing guide names school and agency contracts as a separate payment arrangement SLP practices need to track.

Best for: groups doing a lot of teletherapy that want built in therapy tools. Limits: small per use fees add up at volume, so model them at your claim count.

SimplePractice

SimplePractice is a general therapy EHR used by many speech therapists. Its pricing page lists Starter $49, Essential $79 and Plus $99 a month for one clinician, with added clinicians at $74 each on Plus. Group telehealth and advanced analytics sit in Plus. Groups of 6 or more can ask for custom pricing. Add-ons include Care Aide at $59 a month. SimplePractice often runs introductory discounts, so compare standard prices.

Best for: small groups that want a clean client portal, telehealth and claims in one place. Limits: it is not built only for SLPs, so check goal banks and evaluation templates.

WebPT

WebPT is a rehab EMR for physical, occupational and speech therapists. Its pricing page lists plans from $79 to $249 per provider a month and mentions billing tools such as 8 minute rule and NCCI edits. For more on WebPT and Raintree from the PT side, see our physical therapy EMR guide.

Best for: groups where SLPs work beside PTs and OTs on one system. Limits: PT is the core focus, so test the speech templates.

Raintree

Raintree sells an EMR for rehab and multi discipline therapy clinics, adult and pediatric, from multi site practices to enterprise groups. Pricing is by custom quote. Passage Health's 2026 summary says RCM and business intelligence tools may be quoted separately.

Best for: larger groups with several sites and PT, OT and SLP lines. Limits: no published price, and the full cost depends on modules.

Jane

Jane is scheduling, charting and billing software for allied health clinics. Its pricing page lists Balance at 59, Practice at 79 and Thrive at 99 a month, shown in your region's currency, with add-ons such as AI Scribe at $15 and Group Telehealth at $15 per opted in practitioner. Insurance billing is an add-on. Our practice management software guide covers Jane and other systems across specialties.

Best for: private pay clinics that want strong online booking. Limits: check US insurance billing depth before choosing it for an insurance heavy group.

ClinicNote

ClinicNote is built for speech language pathology, audiology, behavioral health and psychology private practices, plus university clinics. It links scheduling, notes and billing. Pricing: see the vendor's site.

Best for: SLP and audiology practices that want a discipline specific tool. Limits: confirm claim submission, ERA posting and group pricing in a demo.

TheraNest (Ensora)

TheraNest is Ensora's mental health product, priced at $29, $59 or $89 per therapist a month. It shows up in speech therapy searches, but for SLPs, Fusion is the Ensora product to look at.

School and agency contracts

School contracts are where most speech therapy software is weakest. A district or agency usually pays on a monthly invoice for sessions or days, not by claim. Three questions for every vendor:

  1. Can I invoice a district for a batch of sessions across many students, with one total?
  2. Can I report sessions and collections by contract, separate from insurance and private pay?
  3. Can I export that data cleanly to my accountant each month?

If the answer is no, you will track contracts in a spreadsheet, and that is where unbilled sessions and late payments hide.

How to choose

  1. Pediatric group billing insurance and Medicaid: start with Fusion and ClinicSource.
  2. Mostly teletherapy: start with TheraPlatform, then compare SimplePractice.
  3. Small group that wants the fastest setup: SimplePractice.
  4. Mixed PT, OT and SLP group: WebPT, or Raintree at multi site scale.
  5. Private pay clinic: Jane, then SimplePractice.
  6. Several school contracts: ask the three contract questions above before anything else.

Then price it at your size, with all claim, eligibility and add-on fees, for 12 months.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is the best software for a speech therapy group practice?

There is no single best choice. For an insurance heavy pediatric group, Fusion and ClinicSource are the usual first looks. For teletherapy, TheraPlatform. For simple setup, SimplePractice. Test each with your own evaluation and progress note templates.

How much does speech therapy practice software cost?

Published list prices run from about $25 a month for a solo Fusion plan to $249 per provider a month at the top of WebPT's range. For six clinicians, expect a few hundred dollars a month or more, before claim and add-on fees. Raintree and some others quote on request.

Can speech therapy software bill school districts?

Some tools offer invoicing and batching that can work for district billing, but most are built around claims and private pay. Ask the vendor to show a monthly district invoice in a demo. Then decide whether contract revenue lives in the software or in your books.

Is SimplePractice good for speech therapy?

It works for many small groups, with telehealth, a client portal and claims in one system. It is a general therapy EHR, so check SLP goal banks, evaluation templates and group pricing before you commit.

Do I need separate teletherapy software?

Usually not. SimplePractice, TheraPlatform, Fusion (higher plan) and Jane (group add-on) include telehealth. TheraPlatform adds interactive therapy tools that many SLPs use in sessions.

Should my speech therapy group take insurance or go private pay?

It depends on your payer rates, caseload and cash timing. Our insurance versus private pay guide walks through the break even math.

Does practice software replace bookkeeping?

No. It tracks sessions, claims and invoices. Your books track cash in the bank, payroll, expenses and taxes, and the two need to be reconciled every month.
