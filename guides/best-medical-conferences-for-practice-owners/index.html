Short answer

Most practice owners get the most business value from three kinds of events. First, the MGMA conferences (Financial in March, Private Practice in April, Annual in September) for any specialty. Second, the one big specialty meeting that matches your revenue, such as AAD for dermatology, AAO for ophthalmology, Yankee Dental Congress for dental, or AmSpa Medical Spa Show for med spas. Third, a small owner focused event if one exists for your field, such as the APTA Private Practice conference or the iBOSS summit for ABA agencies, and the table below gives dates, cities and costs for 33 events, checked on organizer pages in November 2026.

How the list was built

Each event was checked against the organizer's own page where it could be read. Where 2027 prices or dates are not announced or not published on the organizer page, the table says so. Some events changed: the ADA has stopped SmileCon, the MGMA Leaders Conference is now the MGMA Annual Conference, and Vision Expo East and West are now one show.

Cross specialty practice management

MGMA Financial, Private Practice and Annual Conferences

MGMA is the main association for medical practice management. It runs several events a year. The Financial Conference (Atlanta, Mar 4 to 6, 2027) is all finance. The Private Practice Conference (Tampa, Apr 8 to 10) covers payer contracts, revenue growth and benchmarking. The Annual Conference (Philadelphia, Sept 12 to 15) is the large event, and it is the new name of the Leaders Conference. MGMA also lists an Operations Conference (Seattle, May 2 to 4). Provider organization pricing for the first three runs from $699 to $1,099 depending on how early you register, with group discounts for several attendees. These events suit owners and administrators. They are not clinical meetings. Annual Conference pricing is not published yet. Groups with several locations can see how Lucid supports them on the medical group page.

AAPC HEALTHCON

National Harbor, Mar 14 to 17, 2027. Built around coding and revenue cycle. It fits practices that bill a lot of insurance and want their billing team trained. It is less useful for cash pay practices.

How to choose

  1. Solo or one location with a small team: pick one event, usually your specialty meeting.
  2. Several locations or a growing group: add an MGMA event.
  3. Med spa owners: Boot Camp if you are early or restructuring, Medical Spa Show if you are scaling.
  4. Cash pay or lean practices: skip the biggest shows and consider a day pass or regional meeting.
  5. Owners who must pick two: one clinical meeting and one business event.

How to get the most business value from a conference

  1. Set a budget before you register. Add registration, travel, hotel, meals and the days your practice earns less because you are away, and decide who goes.
  2. Go with a short list. Write down three questions you want answered, for example whether to add a service line, replace a device or open a second location.
  3. Treat the exhibit hall as price discovery. Get written quotes from two vendors for any equipment you are considering. Ask about financing, lease terms and show only pricing, and ask your CPA whether the purchase may qualify for Section 179. Do not sign at the show, and get the quote in writing with a date it stays valid.
  4. Ask the vendor how the purchase will be paid back. A device is only a good idea if the added visits or procedures cover the payment, staff time and supplies. Ask for customer references with a similar practice size.
  5. Tax treatment, in general terms only. Travel and registration for a business conference are often deductible business expenses, with rules on meals, personal travel and documentation. Section 179 can let some businesses deduct qualifying equipment in the year it is put to use, with limits. Confirm every item with your CPA before you buy, and keep receipts, the agenda and notes on the business purpose.
  6. Bring the numbers back within two weeks. Turn each idea into a one page estimate: cost, expected added revenue, extra staff or supply cost, payback period and cash needed. Share it with your CPA or CFO before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Which medical conference is best for a practice owner?

For business content across any specialty, the MGMA Financial Conference and Private Practice Conference are the most direct. For specialty content, choose your specialty's main meeting. Dental owners should look at Yankee Dental Congress, and med spa owners at AmSpa events.

Which conferences cover practice finance?

The MGMA Financial Conference is entirely finance. The MGMA Private Practice Conference covers payer contracts and benchmarking. AAPC HEALTHCON covers coding and revenue cycle. iBOSS covers finance for ABA agency owners.

How much does a medical conference cost?

Registration in this list runs from under $100 for exhibit hall passes to about $3,200 for a non member at the largest specialty meetings. Most owner focused events cost $700 to $1,400. Add travel and hotel.

Are 2027 dates confirmed?

Most are, from organizer pages. Vegas Cosmetic Surgery has no confirmed 2027 dates. Confirm any event on the organizer page before you book. Prices for many 2027 events open in early 2027.

Are conference costs tax deductible?

Often, if the trip has a clear business purpose, but rules cover travel, meals, personal days and records. Equipment bought after a show may qualify for Section 179 or other treatment, with limits. Ask your CPA before you buy.

Do I need a conference for my first practice?

No. A specialty meeting is optional. Many first time owners get more from a small business event, such as a boot camp or the MGMA Private Practice Conference.

Should my whole team go?

Send the people who make buying and payer decisions. Several events charge less for staff, for example optometric staff and dental office professionals.

What should I bring back to my CPA after a conference?

Vendor quotes, financing terms, the service line ideas you want to test and any payer changes you heard about. Lucid turns these into a plan with your dedicated CPA, and with CFO Services if you add them.
