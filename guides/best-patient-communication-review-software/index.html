Short answer

For most private practices the shortlist is Weave, Podium, Birdeye, Solutionreach, RepuGen, NexHealth, Klara, Tebra and Doctible. Weave, Solutionreach and Doctible suit small offices that want texting, reminders and review requests in one place, Birdeye and Podium suit multi location groups and med spas that want reviews, messaging and lead capture together, and RepuGen and Tebra focus on healthcare reviews with HIPAA safe replies. Weave, Solutionreach and Tebra show a starting price, so ask every other vendor for a written quote per location. What to look for first

Four jobs decide most of the result.

  1. Review requests sent automatically after a completed visit, by text, with one tap to the review site.
  2. Two way texting from your office number, covered by a signed business associate agreement (BAA).
  3. A shared inbox, so texts, web chat and review alerts are not split across staff phones.
  4. Safe replies. Public replies must never confirm that someone is a patient or mention care details. Tools that draft replies help, but a person should approve each one.

Then check the basics. Does it read your schedule from your practice management system? Is pricing per location or per provider? What is the contract length?

Weave

Weave combines phones, texting, reviews and payments. Its review pages describe review requests sent right after appointments, with one click to the review page. Its texting pages describe two way texting from the office number, plus bulk texting. Its pricing page says plans start from $199 a month.

Best for: dental, optometry and small medical offices that also want a phone system. Limits: the full price depends on the plan and add ons. Get a written quote for your locations.

Podium

Podium sells lead management, messaging and review tools, with healthcare and med spa versions. Its healthcare page describes improving the patient experience and increasing online reviews. Podium publishes a business associate agreement in its legal center for healthcare clients.

Best for: med spas, aesthetic practices and multi location groups that want leads, texting and reviews together. Limits: plans are bought through its sales team. Check the plan list on its site and ask about contract length and per location fees.

Birdeye

Birdeye serves healthcare brands from single offices to large groups. Its healthcare pages describe review generation with automated requests after appointments, listings management and patient messaging across locations, with AI agents for booking and visibility.

Best for: multi location groups that want one view of reviews and listings across every office. Limits: pricing is not published and is quoted per location. Read the renewal terms before you sign.

Solutionreach

Solutionreach is a patient engagement platform for dental, vision and medical offices. Its pricing page lists review invitations that text patients a link to your Google or Facebook page, along with reminders, recalls, text to pay and team chat. It says pricing starts at $199.

Best for: small offices that want reminders, recalls and reviews from one vendor. Limits: features differ by package and add on, so compare the package chart carefully.

RepuGen

RepuGen is built only for healthcare. Its site lists patient surveys, past patient outreach, multi question surveys, NPS reporting, a testimonial page and AI generated, HIPAA compliant review replies. Pricing is per provider through an estimate form.

Best for: practices that want a healthcare focused review tool and patient feedback surveys. Limits: it is a reputation tool, not a full texting or phone platform.

NexHealth

NexHealth adds reviews to its booking and forms platform. Its help center says it reads your appointment list and sends a feedback survey to patients who completed visits, then guides positive reviewers to Google. It also offers recalls and two way communication.

Best for: offices already using or considering NexHealth for online booking. Limits: pricing is by quote. Review routing must follow Google's rules, so check how it is set up (see the FAQ below).

Klara (ModMed)

Klara, part of ModMed, focuses on patient text messaging. Its pages list automated appointment reminders, scheduling, digital intake and a web messenger, plus tools for staff to work a shared inbox.

Best for: specialty practices that want strong two way messaging, especially on ModMed. Limits: pricing is not published, and review tools are not its main focus.

Tebra (PatientPop)

Tebra, which merged with PatientPop, sells reputation management inside its practice platform. Its pages list automated review requests after appointments, AI suggested review replies, review monitoring across sites and AI Review Insights that group feedback into themes. Its pricing page says pricing ranges from $49 to $799 per provider a month, by solution or bundle.

Best for: independent practices that want reviews tied to their EHR, billing and scheduling. Limits: the best value comes with Tebra's wider platform.

Doctible

Doctible serves dental, vision and medical offices. Its plans page lists a HIPAA compliant website, two way HIPAA compliant texting, appointment reminders, recalls and, on higher plans, reputation management that pulls from your EHR to automate review requests. Its FAQ says most customers pay monthly invoices on annual terms, with month to month also offered.

Best for: small offices that want a website, texting and reviews together. Limits: pricing is by demo and quote.

How to choose

  1. Small office that wants one vendor: compare Weave, Solutionreach and Doctible.
  2. Med spa or aesthetic practice: start with Podium and Birdeye. See also our med spa software guide.
  3. Multi location group: shortlist Birdeye and Podium and ask for per location pricing.
  4. Healthcare only review tool: RepuGen or Tebra.
  5. Dental: Weave, NexHealth and Solutionreach are common. See our dental practice management software guide.

Ask every vendor the same five questions. Will you sign a BAA? Is it priced per location or per provider? What is the contract length and renewal term? Does it read my schedule automatically? Who approves replies before they post?

Reviews and messaging are marketing spend

Review and texting tools are often booked as plain software. In practice they are part of how you win and keep patients, next to ads, an agency or Zocdoc.

  1. Put the tool on its own expense line, by location.
  2. Track new patients by source each month.
  3. Add the tool's cost to your marketing total when you work out what each new patient costs.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is the best review software for a small practice?

There is no single best choice. Weave and Solutionreach publish starting prices and combine reviews with texting and reminders. RepuGen and Tebra focus on healthcare reviews. Compare written quotes for your locations.

Is it HIPAA compliant to text patients?

It can be, if the vendor signs a BAA and staff follow your privacy policy. Weave, Podium, Doctible and Klara describe secure or HIPAA compliant messaging. Keep clinical details out of plain texts unless the tool is set up for them.

How should a practice reply to a bad review?

Thank the reviewer, do not confirm they are a patient and do not mention care or visit details. Invite them to call the office. RepuGen and Tebra offer drafted replies built for healthcare, but a person should approve each one.

Can we only ask happy patients for Google reviews?

No. Google's review policy says businesses must not discourage negative reviews or selectively ask for positive ones, and must not offer incentives. Ask every patient the same way, and check how your tool routes low scores.

How many reviews does a practice need?

There is no fixed number. A steady pace of recent reviews matters more than a large total. Send requests after every completed visit.

What do review tools cost?

Weave lists plans from $199 a month, Solutionreach says pricing starts at $199 and Tebra lists $49 to $799 per provider a month by bundle. Most others quote per location or per provider.

Do review tools also take payments?

Several do, including Weave and Solutionreach. For a full comparison, see our patient payment software guide.
