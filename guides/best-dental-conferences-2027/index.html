Short answer

For a dental owner who cares about the business side, Yankee Dental Congress in Boston (Jan 28 to 30, 2027) is the strongest pick, because it runs an Ownership Track, an office manager program and a Multi-Site Summit on DSO activity. Chicago Midwinter (Feb 25 to 27), the Hinman meeting in Atlanta (Mar 18 to 20), CDA Presents in Anaheim (May 13 to 15) and the Greater New York Dental Meeting (Nov 26 to 30) are the big regional shows for CE and equipment shopping. Send your office manager to AADOM in Scottsdale in September, and watch the ADSO site if you are thinking about a DSO deal. The ADA ended SmileCon after 2025 and has paused its annual meeting, so there is no ADA national show to plan around in 2027.

For medical and other specialty meetings, see our guide to medical conferences for practice owners. If your office manager or administrator runs the business side, see Which practice management conferences are worth it?

What changed: SmileCon and the ADA meeting

The ADA announced in May 2025 that the October 2025 SmileCon in Washington, DC would be the last, saying attendance never returned to pre COVID levels. The ADA's future meetings page now says it has paused its annual meeting, apart from the House of Delegates, until further notice. That is why many owners are choosing a regional meeting instead, and why the regional shows below matter more in 2027.

Yankee Dental Congress: best for owners

Yankee runs Jan 28 to 30, 2027 in Boston. It has the clearest business programming of any 2027 dental meeting:

  • An Ownership Track and a Women in Business program.
  • The Dental Office Manager's Advantage, a program built for office managers.
  • Team Success: Growing Stronger Practices, a team track.
  • The Yankee Multi-Site Summit, sessions on DSO consolidation, mergers and acquisitions, market changes, AI and business systems. Courses are $29 each for one CE hour, or $79 for a full Thursday or Friday (4 CE hours).

General registration covers all three days. ADA member dentists pay $140 in the first window, $170 from Oct 31 to Dec 28, 2026, and $200 after. Nonmember dentists pay $270 to $445. Office and non clinical professionals pay $70 to $125. Refund requests close Dec 28, 2026.

If you can attend only one meeting for the business side, make it this one, and bring your office manager.

The big regional meetings

Chicago Dental Society Midwinter Meeting. Feb 25 to 27, 2027, McCormick Place West, Chicago. It is one of the largest dental meetings in the US, with a very large exhibit hall. Members and nonmembers register through one CDS account, and the CDS applies member or nonmember pricing at checkout. Fees are not listed on the public pages; see the CDS site. Search the course list for business and practice management sessions before you register.

Pacific Dental Conference. Mar 4 to 6, 2027, Vancouver Convention Centre. It offers more than 200 sessions and hands on workshops, in person only. Non BCDA member dentists pay $330 early (until Jan 15), $410 regular and $475 onsite; administrative staff pay $290 to $475. Prices are before 5% GST and are set by a Canadian organizer. It suits owners in the Pacific Northwest and Western Canada.

Thomas P. Hinman Dental Meeting. Mar 18 to 20, 2027, Georgia World Congress Center, Atlanta. Registration is open, but fees are not shown on the meeting's home page. The education list is clinical. It is a strong exhibit hall for the Southeast.

CDA Presents. May 13 to 15, 2027, Anaheim Convention Center. Run by the California Dental Association. Registration opens in January 2027, so fees are not announced yet. Good for California owners who want CE and vendor deals in one trip.

Greater New York Dental Meeting. Nov 26 to 30, 2027, Javits Center, New York, with the exhibit floor open Nov 28 to 30. For the 2026 meeting the organizer offered free pre registration; 2027 terms are not announced. It is the best end of year exhibit hall for equipment shopping, close to year end tax planning. Talk to your CPA before you buy at the show.

Meetings for office managers and DSO leaders

AADOM Conference. The American Association of Dental Office Management holds its 2027 conference at the Fairmont Scottsdale Princess in Scottsdale, Arizona, with education sessions Sept 9 to 11, 2027. Early bird ends Mar 31, 2027: $1,199 for AADOM members and $1,699 for nonmembers. Standard pricing from Apr 1 is $1,399 and $1,799. A $1,599 package adds a three year AADOM membership. Every package includes meals Thursday to Saturday, an evening social event and an exhibit hall of more than 100 companies. This is the meeting for the person who runs your schedule, insurance, hiring and collections.

ADSO Summit. The Association of Dental Support Organizations held its 2026 Summit June 15 to 17 in Chicago, open to members and nonmembers, and its Next Level conference for mid market DSOs Oct 21 to 23, 2026 in Dallas. The ADSO events page has not posted 2027 dates yet. It fits owners who run several offices, plan to, or are weighing a DSO offer. Before any DSO conversation, read should I sell my dental practice to a DSO and how to choose a dental practice broker.

Dental Entrepreneur Woman. DeW held its 10 year retreat Nov 12 to 14, 2026 in Charlotte. A 2027 retreat is not announced.

How to choose

  1. One office, owner does most of the business work: Yankee, or your nearest regional meeting plus a few Yankee Ownership Track courses.
  2. Office manager runs the front desk and collections: AADOM for the manager; Yankee's office manager program if Boston is closer.
  3. Two or more offices, or growth by acquisition: the Yankee Multi-Site Summit, and the ADSO Summit once 2027 dates post.
  4. Buying a big piece of equipment: pick the closest large exhibit hall (Midwinter, Hinman, CDA Presents, Pacific or Greater New York), and get quotes before you travel so you can compare.
  5. Thinking about selling: DSO sessions are useful, but the decision starts with clean books and a clear view of your profit, not with a conference.

How to bring the value home

Set a budget first, including the clinical days you lose while the office is closed or short staffed. Go with three questions, for example whether to add implants or clear aligners, replace a scanner or CBCT, open a second office or drop a PPO. In the exhibit hall, get two written quotes, ask about lease terms and show pricing, and do not sign on the floor. Within two weeks, turn each idea into a one page estimate: cost, added production, staff time, supplies, cash needed and payback period. Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Is SmileCon happening in 2027?

No. The ADA held the final SmileCon in Washington, DC in October 2025 and has paused its annual meeting, apart from the House of Delegates, until further notice.

Which dental conference is best for the business side?

Yankee Dental Congress, because it runs an Ownership Track, a Women in Business program, an office manager program and the Multi-Site Summit on DSO activity. AADOM is the best pick for office managers.

How much does a dental conference cost in 2027?

Most regional meetings cost a few hundred dollars for a dentist: Yankee runs $140 to $445 and Pacific $330 to $475 before tax. AADOM costs $1,199 to $1,799 because meals and events are included. Several 2027 fees, including Hinman and CDA Presents, are not announced yet.

Should my office manager go to a conference?

Yes, if they run scheduling, insurance and collections. AADOM is built for them, and Yankee has a dedicated office manager program with staff rates from $70.

Which dental conferences cover DSOs and practice transitions?

The Yankee Multi-Site Summit covers DSO consolidation and acquisitions, and the ADSO Summit is the main DSO industry event. ADSO has not posted 2027 dates yet.

Is it smart to buy equipment at a dental show?

Shows are good for price discovery, and some offer show pricing. Get written quotes, compare at least two vendors, and model the payback with your CPA before you sign.

Are dental conference costs tax deductible?

Often, when the trip has a clear business purpose, but rules cover meals, personal days and records. Equipment may qualify for Section 179, with limits. Ask your CPA before you buy.
