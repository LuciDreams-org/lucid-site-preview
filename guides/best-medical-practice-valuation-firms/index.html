Which firms value medical practices, and at what cost?

Short answer

Healthcare specialist firms such as VMG Health, Stout (which now includes HealthCare Appraisers) and HealthFMV suit groups with surgery centers, management services structures or compliance questions. For a single practice, a credentialed appraiser such as Nechay Advisors in California, Cooper Norman in Idaho and Utah, Peak Business Valuation or BizWorth gives an independent report, and dental owners can add Henry Schein Dental Practice Transitions or Dentappraisal. Published fees we found run from about $3,000 to $20,000 for a report, with full litigation grade reports at the top. Whoever you pick, the value is built on your books, so get them clean and reviewed first.

This guide compares who does the work and what it costs. For the methods themselves (multiples, EBITDA and add backs), see how a medical practice is valued.

When you need a formal valuation

A rough estimate is fine for planning. A formal written report from a credentialed appraiser is usually needed when someone else has to rely on the number.

Selling the practiceTests a buyer's offer and sets your asking rangeSummary or full report
Partner buy in or buyoutSets the price under your buy sell agreementSummary or full report
DivorceA court may rely on it and the other side may challenge itFull, litigation ready report
Gift or estateThe IRS may review the value of the sharesFull report from a qualified appraiser
SBA loan to buy a practiceThe lender needs an independent valueReport from a qualified source the lender accepts
Compensation or management feesFair market value support for regulatory rulesFair market value opinion

Nechay Advisors, for example, says its detailed report is the one required for divorce, shareholder disputes and gift and estate tax, while its summary report fits sales, buy ins and SBA loans. Cooper Norman lists partner buy ins and buyouts, sales and mergers, gifts and estates, and disputes as the common triggers.

SBA rules changed on October 1, 2026. Lender and law firm summaries of the new SBA guidance say most change of ownership deals now need an independent valuation from a qualified source, such as an ASA, CBA, ABV or CVA holder. Ask your lender what it will accept before you order a report, and read the practice acquisition loans guide.

Credentials to look for

  1. ASA (Accredited Senior Appraiser), from the American Society of Appraisers.
  2. CVA (Certified Valuation Analyst), from NACVA, the National Association of Certified Valuators and Analysts.
  3. ABV (Accredited in Business Valuation), from the AICPA, held by CPAs.
  4. CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst), common in larger valuation teams.
  5. CHBC (Certified Healthcare Business Consultant), a healthcare business credential some appraisers add.

Many appraisers also say their reports follow USPAP, the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice. Ask which standard your report follows and who signs it.

Healthcare specialist firms

VMG Health

VMG Health is a healthcare only valuation, transaction and strategy firm. Its business valuation pages cover mergers, joint ventures and fair market value opinions for complex arrangements. Its team bios list CFA, CVA, ABV and ASA holders.

Best for: larger groups, surgery center partnerships and deals where fair market value must hold up to regulators.

Stout, including HealthCare Appraisers

HealthCare Appraisers is now part of Stout, and the HealthCare Appraisers site points to Stout's healthcare valuation page. That page describes independent fair market value and commercial reasonableness opinions, a fixed monthly fee partnership for compensation valuation projects, and automated fair market value calculators, including one for physical therapy multiples.

Best for: groups with ongoing compensation and compliance valuation needs.

HealthFMV

HealthFMV is a healthcare valuation and transaction firm led by Will Hamilton, a CVA through NACVA with more than 15 years of healthcare valuation and M&A experience, per its site. Its listed work includes buy ins and buyouts of owners, gift and estate tax, shareholder disputes and management services fee arrangements.

Best for: an owner who wants a healthcare only appraiser, or a practice with a management services structure.

Independent appraisers for a single practice

Nechay Advisors

Nechay Advisors values and sells medical practices and surgery centers in California. Its site says every appraisal is prepared by Alexey Nechay, CVA, follows USPAP and reports more than 500 valuations across more than 45 specialties. It is one of the few firms that publishes prices: summary reports start at $3,950 and detailed reports at $7,500, with hourly consulting for a second opinion on an offer. Most engagements run one to two weeks, per its site. It also offers brokerage.

Best for: California owners who want a published price and a credentialed report.

Cooper Norman

Cooper Norman is a CPA firm in Idaho and Utah with a healthcare valuation page. Its partner roster names Jordan Graves, CPA, ABV, CFF and Matthew Barker, CPA, CVA. It explains the difference between a calculation of value (methods agreed up front, fewer steps) and a full valuation, and says the engagement letter states scope and fee before work begins.

Best for: practices in Idaho and Utah that want a valuation from a CPA firm.

Eton Venture Services

Eton Venture Services is a valuation and M&A advisory firm that publishes a guide to healthcare valuation firms. It describes its own pricing as flat fee engagements set by scope and complexity, with a quote on request.

Best for: owners who want a fixed fee for a healthcare transaction valuation.

Peak Business Valuation

Peak Business Valuation is a national business appraisal firm with a med spa valuation page. Its cost guide names ABV and CVA as the credentials to look for and lists rush fees, equipment appraisals and expert witness work as extras. Its FAQ says an appraisal typically costs $3,000 to $20,000, depending on purpose, size and detail.

Best for: med spas and smaller practices that want a published fee range and an SBA ready report.

BizWorth

BizWorth sells valuation reports online, with tiers for small sales, standard sales, SBA loans, lender prequalification and a certified report for litigation, IRS and partner disputes. Its team bios list CVA, CPA, MAFF and ECA holders, and it says reports are completed by NACVA certified appraisers. Pricing is shown instantly online.

Best for: a straightforward sale or SBA loan where speed and a clear price matter.

Dental valuation firms

Henry Schein Dental Practice Transitions

Henry Schein Dental Practice Transitions is a dental brokerage and valuation firm and a subsidiary of Henry Schein. It says it has completed about 2,000 dental practice valuations in the last five years, for sales, associate buy ins, mergers, partnerships, financing and letters of instruction for death or disability.

Best for: dental owners planning a sale through a broker, or an associate buy in.

Dentappraisal

Dentappraisal provides independent dental practice valuations nationwide, offering an opinion of value or a calculation of value. It says each report is completed by its CPA, and it suggests owners get a valuation every three to five years.

Best for: dental buyers and sellers who want a valuation from a firm that is not brokering the sale.

Independent report or broker estimate?

Many brokers offer a free valuation as the first step toward listing your practice. That can be useful for a ballpark. A paid, independent report is the safer choice when a partner, a court, the IRS or a lender will rely on the number. Ask any firm whether it also earns a fee if you sell.

To pick a broker, read Which medical practice brokers sell small practices? or, for dental, How do I choose a dental practice broker?

How to choose

  1. Name the purpose first. Sale, buy in, divorce, estate or loan each need a different report.
  2. Match the firm to your size. Surgery centers, management services structures and large groups fit a healthcare specialist. A single practice often fits an independent appraiser.
  3. Check the credential and the standard. Ask who signs the report and whether it holds an ASA, CVA or ABV.
  4. Get the fee and timeline in writing. Ask what is included, what costs extra, and whether testimony is billed separately.
  5. Ask for a sample report from a practice like yours.
  6. Have your books ready. A valuation is only as good as the financials behind it, which is why many sellers also get a quality of earnings review.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How much does a medical practice valuation cost?

Published fees we found run from about $3,000 to $20,000. Nechay Advisors lists summary reports from $3,950 and detailed reports from $7,500, and Peak Business Valuation's FAQ says $3,000 to $20,000. Larger healthcare firms quote by scope.

Who should value my practice before I sell or bring in a partner?

An independent appraiser with an ASA, CVA or ABV credential and healthcare experience. For a group with surgery centers or management services fees, a healthcare specialist such as VMG Health, Stout or HealthFMV. For a single practice, an independent appraiser often fits.

Do I need a formal valuation for a partner buy in?

Usually yes, unless your buy sell agreement sets a formula both sides accept. A written report protects both the buyer and the existing owners. Your healthcare attorney should review the agreement terms.

What kind of valuation do I need for a divorce or an estate?

A full, detailed report from a credentialed appraiser, because a court or the IRS may review it. Nechay Advisors, for example, says its detailed report is the one required in those cases. Ask your attorney or estate advisor which standard applies.

Is a free broker valuation good enough?

It can be a fair starting point for a sale. A broker who earns a fee when you sell is not independent, so use a paid report when someone else will rely on the number.

How long does a practice valuation take?

Nechay Advisors says most of its engagements run one to two weeks once it has your documents. Missing or messy records slow every firm down.

What documents does the valuation firm need?

Expect three to five years of profit and loss statements, balance sheets and tax returns, plus payer mix, provider production, owner pay and any one time expenses. Clean monthly books with documented add backs make the work faster and the value easier to defend.
