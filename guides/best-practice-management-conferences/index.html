Which practice management conferences are worth it?

Short answer

For most medical groups, the MGMA events are the safest choice: the Financial Conference in March, the Private Practice Conference in April, the Operations Conference in May and the MGMA Annual Conference in September, which is the new name of the MGMA Leaders Conference. Add AAPC HEALTHCON if your team owns coding and billing, or an HFMA event if your role is finance and revenue cycle. Specialty administrator meetings such as AAOE for orthopedics, ASOA for ophthalmology and RBMA for radiology are often the best value per dollar. State MGMA conferences cost a few hundred dollars and are the cheapest way to build a local peer network.

This page is for the people who run the business side: owners, administrators, COOs and practice managers. For clinical specialty meetings, see our guide to medical conferences for practice owners. For dental meetings, see Which dental conferences should owners attend in 2027?

MGMA: Leaders, Annual and the spring events

MGMA is the main association for medical practice management, and its conferences are not clinical meetings. Each one is built for a role.

MGMA Annual Conference (formerly the Leaders Conference). If you searched for the MGMA Leaders Conference, this is it. MGMA now calls it the Annual Conference. The 2027 meeting runs Sept 12 to 15 in Philadelphia. Registration opens in spring 2027, and prices are not announced yet. It is the largest MGMA event and the broadest: operations, finance, people, technology and policy in one place. It suits administrators of larger groups and anyone who wants the widest peer network.

MGMA Financial Conference. Atlanta, Mar 4 to 6, 2027. MGMA describes it as an event for healthcare financial leaders building financially sustainable practices. Attendees from provider organizations pay $699 through Jan 7, $899 through Feb 9 and $1,099 after that. Attendees from non provider organizations pay $1,399 to $1,799. If your job is budgets, cash and payer revenue, start here.

MGMA Private Practice Conference. Tampa, Apr 8 to 10, 2027. It is aimed at private practice leaders protecting their independence and growing the bottom line. Provider organization rates are $699 through Jan 21, $899 through Mar 4 and $1,099 through Apr 10.

MGMA Operations Conference. Seattle, May 2 to 4, 2027. Built for operations leaders: schedule capacity, workflow and staffing. Provider organization rates are $699 through Feb 4, $899 through Mar 31 and $1,099 through May 2.

Team pricing. All three spring events take 15% off for 2 to 3 passes and 30% off for 4 or more. Each pass includes one in depth workshop and a free pass to the MGMA Summit digital event. For a group sending an owner, an administrator and a billing lead, the team discount is worth more than early bird timing alone.

Is MGMA worth it? For most groups with staff, yes, if you pick one event that matches the job you need done. Sending the same person to all four is rarely worth it. MGMA also publishes benchmark data, which is the other reason administrators join. See our guide to practice benchmarking sources.

AAPC HEALTHCON: for the revenue cycle team

AAPC HEALTHCON 2027 runs Mar 14 to 17 in National Harbor, near Washington, DC, with a virtual option. It offers 18 or more CEUs. The registration page lists $1,295 for the onsite or virtual event and $895 as a limited time member price. Cancellations are free until Feb 12, 2027, and a $250 fee applies from Feb 13 to Feb 28.

It is the right pick when your coders and billers need continuing education credits and the latest coding changes. It is less useful for an owner who wants strategy. AAPC also runs HEALTHCON Regional in Louisville, Sept 20 to 22, 2027 (15 CEUs, hybrid), and virtual only events: RISKCON (June 8 to 9), DOCUCON (Aug 3 to 4) and REVCON (Nov 9 to 10). If your billing runs through software rather than a team, see our RCM software guide.

HFMA: for finance and revenue cycle leaders

HFMA is the Healthcare Financial Management Association. Its audience leans toward hospitals and health systems, but large medical groups send finance staff too.

  • Revenue Cycle Conference: Mar 10 to 12, 2027, Orlando. HFMA expects more than 600 revenue cycle professionals. Registration is open. HFMA also runs an application program called Executive Connections that can give qualified provider executives complimentary registration in exchange for vendor meetings.
  • Leadership Summit: Apr 18 to 20, 2027, Atlanta. For finance leaders building leadership skills.
  • Annual Conference: June 27 to 30, 2027, Denver. HFMA's largest event. Prices are not announced yet. Future years are listed in San Antonio (2028) and Nashville (2029).

HFMA also runs local chapter events across the country, which are cheaper and closer to home. Choose HFMA over MGMA when the person going is a controller or revenue cycle director and the questions are about payer contracts, denials and cash, not daily operations.

Specialty practice management meetings

These are often the best value, because every person in the room runs the same kind of practice.

AAOE Annual Conference (orthopedics). Fort Worth, Apr 17 to 20, 2027, with a pre conference day on Apr 17 and the expo hall on Apr 18 to 19. AAOE members pay $499 through Dec 16, $549 through Feb 15, $649 through Mar 31 and $799 after. Nonmember practice professionals pay $849 to $1,149. Registration includes recordings and session summaries. AAOE says that when the managing provider of a practice registers, the administrator can attend free with a discount code, and group rates start at three people. AAOE also runs a Women in Orthopaedic Practice Management (WiOPM) conference returning in fall 2027; details are not announced.

ASOA at the ASCRS Annual Meeting (ophthalmology). San Diego, Apr 2 to 5, 2027. The meeting runs an ASOA program for ophthalmic administrators next to the surgical program. Early bird rates end Jan 22. This is separate from the AAO's practice management program covered in the conferences guide.

RBMA PaRADigm (radiology). Denver, Apr 5 to 8, 2027, at the Hyatt Regency Denver at Colorado Convention Center. The agenda is live. Built for radiology business managers.

Other specialties. The live conferences guide already covers the business programs inside dermatology (AAD), physical therapy (APTA Private Practice), med spa (AmSpa Boot Camp) and ABA (iBOSS) meetings, so they are not repeated here.

Regional and state events

State MGMA chapters run one or two conferences a year. They are short, cheap and full of managers from practices near you, which makes them good for hiring leads, payer gossip and vendor references.

  • California MGMA: May 5 to 7, 2027, DoubleTree by Hilton Palm Springs, Cathedral City. Early rates are $225 for chapter members, $300 for national MGMA members (includes one year of chapter membership), $275 for other state MGMA members and $445 for nonmembers. Three or more members from one organization pay $175 each.
  • Missouri MGMA: May 5 to 7, 2027, Oasis Hotel, Springfield. Details to come.
  • Tennessee MGMA: Spring Conference Apr 28 to 30, 2027, Gatlinburg; Fall Conference Sept 16 to 17, 2027, Franklin.
  • Oregon MGMA: half day Medford Summit, Jan 28, 2027.

MGMA keeps a state events calendar on its site. Two other regional options are worth watching. SVMIC runs a Practice Management Symposium in Franklin, TN; the 2026 edition was Oct 8 to 9 at $199, and 2027 is not announced. KZA held its Ascend practice management summit online on Nov 6, 2026 at $695; a 2027 edition is not announced.

How to choose

  1. One location, small team: a state MGMA conference plus one webinar series. Spend the difference on a good consultant day if you need one. See how to choose a practice consultant.
  2. Growing group with an administrator: one MGMA spring event that matches the role, and register as a team for the discount.
  3. Several locations or an executive team: the MGMA Annual Conference for the network, plus your specialty administrator meeting.
  4. Heavy insurance billing: add AAPC HEALTHCON for the billing lead.
  5. A finance hire or controller: the MGMA Financial Conference or an HFMA event.
  6. Orthopedics, ophthalmology or radiology: your specialty administrator meeting first. It is usually more specific than any general event.

How to bring the value home

A conference pays off only if something changes after it. Set a budget first: registration, travel, hotel and the days your administrator is out. Go with three questions, such as whether to add a service line, move to a new system or open a location. Treat the exhibit hall as price discovery: get two written quotes, do not sign at the show, and ask how the purchase pays back. Then, within two weeks, turn each idea into a one page estimate of cost, added revenue, staff time, cash needed and payback period. If financing is part of the idea, see our equipment financing guide. Registration and travel for a business conference are often deductible, with rules on meals and personal days; check with your CPA and see tax deductions for practice owners.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Is the MGMA Leaders Conference still running?

Yes, under a new name. MGMA now calls it the MGMA Annual Conference. The 2027 event runs Sept 12 to 15 in Philadelphia, and registration opens in spring 2027.

Which MGMA conference is best for a practice owner?

The Private Practice Conference is the closest fit for independent owners. Choose the Financial Conference if your main questions are cash, budgets and payer revenue. The Annual Conference is best for the widest network.

How much does a practice management conference cost?

In this list, registration runs from about $175 at a state chapter meeting to $1,799 for a non provider attendee at MGMA. Most national events cost $700 to $1,300 for practice staff. Travel and hotel often double the total.

Is AAPC HEALTHCON worth it for a practice manager?

It is worth it if you manage coders and billers who need CEUs. It is less useful for strategy, finance or operations questions, where MGMA is the better fit.

Are state MGMA conferences worth it?

Usually, yes, for the price. You meet managers who deal with the same local payers and labor market. They are also the cheapest way to train a new manager.

Should the owner or the administrator go?

Send the person who will act on what they learn. Some events reward sending both: AAOE says the administrator can attend free when the managing provider registers.

Are conference costs tax deductible?

Often, when the trip has a clear business purpose, but rules cover travel, meals, personal days and records. Keep the agenda and receipts and ask your CPA.

What should I bring back to my CPA after a conference?

Vendor quotes, financing terms, service line ideas, staffing changes and any payer changes you heard about. Your CPA or CFO can then test each one against your real numbers.
