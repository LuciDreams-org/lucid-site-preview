In house bookkeeper or online service?

A practice with one location and low volume can run on an in house bookkeeper, but the cost goes beyond a salary. You also carry payroll taxes, benefits, software, backup for time off and review of the work. One person also cannot be your bookkeeper, tax planner and CFO.

An online service gives you a team, software and a monthly close for a fixed fee, with no hiring. The tradeoff is that general services may not understand payer deposits, production and collections, or provider level profit.

Lucid is the middle path for practices that want a specialist team without a hire. Your dedicated CPA and account manager work in your Slack channel with 24/7 support, books are reconciled against payer deposits, and you see profit by provider, location or service line.

Alternatives to Pilot and Bench for a medical practice

Pilot and Bench are good at simple small business books. A medical practice usually needs more: payer deposits reconciled, revenue split by service line, several locations rolled up, and a CPA who knows the specialty. The options above split into three groups.

  1. Medical specialist team with a posted price: Lucid.
  2. General online services: Bookkeeper360, 1-800Accountant, Xendoo.
  3. Large firms for audit and advisory work: CLA, Aprio, PYA.

If you are leaving Pilot or Bench, Lucid handles the switch and cleans up prior months, with the catch up scoped first and a fixed price. Most practices are live in about 24 hours.
