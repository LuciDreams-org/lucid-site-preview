2027 tax deadlines for medical practices

Most medical practice and med spa owners have three big federal tax dates in 2027: February 1 for W-2s and 1099s, March 15 for 2026 S corp and partnership returns, and April 15 for personal returns, C corp returns and the first estimated tax payment for 2027.

Key points

  • You'll send W-2s and Form 1099-NEC by Monday, February 1, 2027.
  • If your practice is an S corp or has more than one owner, you'll file its return by March 15.
  • For 2026 payments, you'll generally send a 1099-NEC only to people you paid $2,000 or more.
  • With an extension, you get six more months to file, not to pay.

What are the 2027 federal tax deadlines for a medical practice?

Your 2027 federal deadlines run from January 15, 2027 to January 18, 2028. Most owners need only the rows that match their practice: whether they have partners, staff or contractors, and whether the IRS taxes the practice as an S corp or a C corp.

Date (2027) What's due Who it applies to
Fri, Jan 15 Last estimated payment for 2026 Estimated tax payers
Mon, Feb 1 W-2s to staff and the Social Security Administration; Form 1099-NEC to contractors and the IRS; Form 941 for the last quarter of 2026; Form 940 Practices with staff or contractors
Mon, Mar 1 Form 1099-MISC to the IRS on paper (March 31 electronically) Practices that paid rent to an individual, or paid a provider's corporation for medical services
Mon, Mar 15 Form 1120-S; Form 1065; K-1s to owners; Form 7004 to extend either; Form 2553 for an S corp election for 2027 S corps and practices with more than one owner
Thu, Apr 15 Form 1040 with Schedule C; Form 1120 (C corp); first estimated payment for 2027; Form 4868; last day for 2026 IRA and HSA contributions Every owner
Fri, Apr 30 Form 941, first quarter Practices with staff
Tue, Jun 15 Second estimated payment Estimated tax payers
Mon, Aug 2 Form 941, second quarter Practices with staff
Wed, Sep 15 Third estimated payment; extended Form 1120-S and Form 1065 Estimated tax payers; extended S corps and partnerships
Fri, Oct 15 Extended Form 1040 and Form 1120; SEP IRA contributions if you extended Owners and C corps that extended
Mon, Nov 1 Form 941, third quarter Practices with staff
Wed, Dec 15 Fourth estimated payment for a C corp C corps
Tue, Jan 18, 2028 Last estimated payment for 2027 Estimated tax payers (individuals)

When a deadline falls on a weekend or holiday, the IRS moves it to the next business day. The IRS lists every date in Publication 509.

What do the forms and terms on this calendar mean?

Estimated tax is the tax you pay the IRS four times a year on income nobody withholds tax from, such as your share of the practice's profit. Form 1120-S is the federal tax return for an S corp. An S corp election is the choice you make on Form 2553 to have your PLLC, LLC or corporation taxed as an S corp. A Schedule K-1 is the form a partnership or S corp gives each owner to show their share of the profit. Form 941 is the quarterly return you file as an employer to report wages and the tax you withheld. Form 1099-NEC is the form you send a contractor, and file with the IRS, to report what you paid them.

Which 2027 tax dates apply to your practice?

Your dates depend on how the IRS taxes your practice. If you own it alone, you report its profit on your personal return, due April 15. If you have partners or an S corp, you file a business return by March 15. If you have staff, you'll add the payroll dates; if you have contractors, you'll add February 1.

How the IRS taxes your practice Business return Your personal return Other dates
Single-owner PLLC or LLC (the default) None; profit goes on Schedule C Form 1040, April 15 Four estimated payments
PLLC or LLC with more than one owner (the default) Form 1065, March 15 Form 1040 with your K-1, April 15 Each owner's estimated payments
S corp Form 1120-S, March 15 Form 1040 with your K-1, April 15 Payroll dates; estimated payments
C corp Form 1120, April 15 Form 1040, April 15 The corporation's estimated payments; payroll dates

If you work in your S corp, the IRS expects you to pay yourself a reasonable salary through payroll; see setting a reasonable owner salary in a medical S corp.

For S corp tax treatment in 2027, you'll generally file Form 2553 by March 15. Still deciding? Read choosing between a PLLC and an S corp.

When are payroll tax forms due if you have staff?

If you run payroll, including your own salary as an S corp owner, you'll file Form 941 four times in 2027: February 1, April 30, August 2 and November 1. You'll also file Form 940 and send W-2s to staff and the Social Security Administration by February 1.

Form 941 is only the report. You or your payroll company deposit the withheld tax on a monthly or semiweekly schedule, set out in IRS Publication 15, the Employer's Tax Guide. If you use a payroll company, ask which forms it files for you.

Who needs a 1099 from your practice in 2027?

By February 1, 2027, you'll send Form 1099-NEC to each contractor you paid $2,000 or more for services in 2026, and file a copy with the IRS. For earlier years the amount was $600. That often includes contract providers, your medical director if you pay them outside payroll, your attorney, and a freelance biller.

You usually don't send a 1099 to a corporation, but you do send one to your attorney, even when the law firm is a corporation (IRS instructions for Form 1099-NEC). You also send Form 1099-MISC to a corporation, including a professional corporation, that you paid $2,000 or more for medical or health care services. If you pay a staffing agency for locum providers, ask your CPA what the agency needs from you.

If patients pay by card, your card processor will send you a Form 1099-K for any amount. A payment app or patient financing platform generally sends one only when you received more than $20,000 across more than 200 transactions. Match it to your books, not your bank deposits, because it shows sales before fees and refunds.

What does a tax extension move, and what doesn't it?

With an extension, you get six more months to file your return. It doesn't give you more time to pay. If you owe tax, you pay what you expect to owe by the original due date, or the IRS charges a late payment penalty and interest on the unpaid amount.

  • File Form 7004 by March 15 to file Form 1120-S or Form 1065 by September 15. A C corp files Form 7004 by April 15 to file Form 1120 by October 15.
  • File Form 4868 by April 15 to file your Form 1040 by October 15.

If you extend an S corp or partnership return, your co-owners will likely need to extend too. Many states offer a PTET election, which lets a PLLC or S corp pay state income tax at the business level, and some set their own deadline for it.

How much does a missed tax deadline cost?

If you file an S corp or partnership return late, the IRS charges a fixed amount for each owner for each month, even when nobody owes tax. If you pay personal tax late, the IRS charges 0.5% of the unpaid tax each month, plus interest. If you also file late without an extension, you'll pay far more.

Example 1: a late S corp return. A med spa taxed as an S corp has two owners and owes no federal income tax for 2026. The return is due March 15, 2027. The IRS counts each month or part of a month late, up to 12, at $260 per owner per month, the amount the IRS set for returns due in 2027.

What the owners do Months late Penalty math Penalty
File Form 7004 by March 15, then the return by September 15 0 None $0
Skip the extension, file on June 10, 2027 3 $260 x 2 owners x 3 $1,560
Skip the extension, file in April 2028 12 (the cap) $260 x 2 owners x 12 $6,240

Example 2: a late personal tax payment. A single-owner PLLC owner still owes $24,000 on their 2026 Form 1040 after estimated payments. The return and payment are due April 15, 2027.

What the owner does Late filing penalty Late payment penalty Total, before interest
Files Form 4868 and pays $24,000 by April 15 $0 $0 $0
Files Form 4868 by April 15, pays on October 15 $0 0.5% x 6 months, $720 $720
No extension, files and pays on October 15 4.5% x 5 months, $5,400 0.5% x 6 months, $720 $6,120

The IRS charges 5% a month for filing late, up to 25%, less the late payment penalty in months when both apply (failure to file; failure to pay). It also charges interest from April 15.

What should you do if your books are behind?

Start in January, not March, because nobody can finish your return until your books are closed for the year. If you file an extension on time and pay what you expect to owe by the original date, you'll avoid most of the penalties shown above.

  1. Find what's missing: which months aren't reconciled and which returns you haven't filed.
  2. Close the books for 2026 first.
  3. File an extension on time if you won't make the deadline.
  4. Pay what you expect to owe by the original date, even before your numbers are final.
  5. Catch up past years. You may have unclaimed refunds; see how far back a medical practice can claim a tax refund.

What should med spa and dental practice owners watch for?

Med spa and dental practice owners often pay providers as contractors, so February 1 is the date they're most likely to miss. If you pay a medical director, a nurse injector or an associate dentist outside payroll, you'll usually owe them a 1099-NEC. If you sell retail products, your state sets its own sales tax dates.

  • Med spa owners: collect a W-9 from your medical director and each contract injector before the first payment. See bookkeeping for med spas.
  • Dental practice owners: if you pay an associate dentist or a hygienist per day as a contractor, you'll send them a 1099-NEC. See bookkeeping for dental practices.

Questions practice owners ask about 2027 tax deadlines

This guide is general information, not tax advice for your practice. Tax rules change and depend on your state and your numbers. Talk to a CPA before you act on it.

When is the S corp tax return due in 2027?

Form 1120-S for a calendar-year S corp is due Monday, March 15, 2027, with each owner's K-1. With Form 7004 filed by then, you'll have until September 15. If your S corp uses a fiscal year, it's due the 15th day of the third month after the year ends.

When are 1099s due in 2027, and do I send one to my medical director?

Send Form 1099-NEC to each contractor and to the IRS by Monday, February 1, 2027. If you pay your medical director a fee outside payroll, you'll usually send them one, even for a flat monthly fee. The IRS gives no automatic extension for Form 1099-NEC, so collect W-9s early.

When are quarterly estimated taxes due in 2027?

Your 2027 payments are due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2027, and January 18, 2028. If you're an S corp owner on payroll, you can raise your withholding to cover some of your estimates. For the amounts, read Lucid's post on estimated tax safe harbors for medical practice owners.

Does an extension give me more time to pay?

No. On a personal return with Form 4868, if you've paid at least 90% of your tax by April 15 and pay the rest by the extended date, the IRS generally won't charge the late payment penalty, though it still charges interest on the balance.

What happens if I file my S corp return late?

The IRS charges a penalty for each owner for each month or part of a month the return is late, up to 12 months, even if the S corp owes no tax. Your co-owners can't file without their K-1s. If you've filed on time in past years, ask your CPA whether you can ask the IRS to remove the penalty.

My books are months behind. Can I still file on time?

Often, yes, if you start in January. Gather your bank and card statements, payroll reports and the 1099s you've received, then close 2026. If you won't finish in time, file an extension and pay what you expect to owe. Lucid can catch up your books for a fixed price.
