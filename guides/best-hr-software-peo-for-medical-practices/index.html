Short answer

A practice that wants group health benefits and someone else to carry HR compliance should price a PEO: Justworks posts its prices, while TriNet, Insperity, ADP TotalSource, Paychex and Rippling PEO quote. A practice that already has a benefits broker and an office manager who handles HR usually pays less with HR software such as Gusto, BambooHR, Paycor or Rippling. Whichever you choose, track clinical license and certification expiry dates yourself, because payer credentialing is a separate job. PEO or HR software: the real difference

A PEO (professional employer organization) becomes the co-employer of your staff. It runs payroll and tax filing under its own accounts, offers its large-group health plan, handles workers' compensation and gives you HR advisors. You still hire, schedule, set pay and direct clinical work. HR software leaves you as the only employer. It gives you the tools for records, onboarding, time off and reporting, and you buy benefits through a broker.

The U.S. Chamber of Commerce, as cited by Rippling, puts PEO fees at $40 to $200 per employee per month, or 2 to 12 percent of gross payroll. HR software for a 25-person practice often runs a few hundred dollars a month before payroll.

A PEO tends to pay off when:

  1. You have 5 to 50 staff and small-group health quotes are high or thin.
  2. No one in the office owns HR, handbooks or state leave rules.
  3. You hire in several states and do not want to register in each one yourself.

HR software tends to win when:

  1. You already have a broker and a health plan your staff like.
  2. You have an office manager or HR lead who keeps policies current.
  3. You are a larger group where per-employee PEO fees add up fast.

Licensing and credential tracking

Clinical staff licenses, DEA registrations, CPR and BLS cards expire on their own schedules. An expired license can mean a clinician should not be seeing patients. HR tools help here, but differently.

  1. Custom fields. Most HR platforms, including BambooHR and Paycor, let you add fields for license number, state and expiry date, per a 2026 healthcare HR guide. Ask each vendor to show expiry alerts in the demo.
  2. Document storage. Keep copies of licenses and certifications on the employee record, with access limited to HR.
  3. Payer credentialing is separate. Getting a clinician in network with insurers is a different process with its own timeline and fees. See which credentialing services are worth paying for.

A PEO's HR team does not usually track clinical licenses for you. Ask before you assume it does.

Justworks

Justworks is one of few PEOs with a posted price. A third party check of its pricing page on October 27, 2026 lists Payroll at $50 a month plus $8 per employee, PEO Basic at $79 per employee and PEO Plus at $124 per employee, with no base fee on the PEO plans. PEO Plus adds medical, dental and vision administration plus HSA and FSA accounts. A PEO plan needs at least two W-2 employees.

Best for: small practices that want PEO benefits and HR support at a known price. Limits: premiums are on top of the fee, so compare the full monthly cost.

TriNet

TriNet sells a PEO and a lighter HR Plus service. Its pricing page asks for company size, location and services, then gives a quote. It does not publish standard prices.

Best for: growing groups that want a large PEO with industry focused benefit options. Limits: quote only, so get two other PEO quotes for comparison.

Insperity

Insperity's pricing page says its service is not one size fits all and neither are its quotes. Insperity is known for assigning HR specialists to each client.

Best for: practices that want hands-on HR advice, not just software. Limits: quote only. Ask for the fee and the benefit premiums as separate lines.

ADP TotalSource

ADP TotalSource is ADP's PEO for small and midsize businesses. ADP says it brings payroll, benefits administration, workers' compensation and compliance together with dedicated HR expertise. ADP quotes every client, and third party guides describe pricing set as a percent of payroll.

Best for: practices that want a very large provider and may later move to ADP's own payroll. Limits: a percent of payroll fee rises with raises and bonuses, so model it at next year's payroll.

Paychex PEO

Paychex runs a PEO covering payroll and tax filing, benefits, HR compliance and workers' compensation, with a dedicated client team. Pricing is quoted. Paychex also owns Paycor, listed below.

Best for: practices that want local reps and a choice of PEO or software later. Limits: quote only.

Rippling

Rippling sells HR, payroll, benefits and IT tools as modules on one employee record. Its pricing says plans start at $8 per employee per month, with a base fee and modules on top. Rippling PEO is quoted, and a third party review notes you can leave the PEO and keep the same system.

Best for: groups adding locations or states that want an exit path from a PEO. Limits: the starting price is not the full price. Get a quote with every module you need.

Gusto

Gusto is payroll and HR software, not a PEO. Per a third party check on November 24, 2026, Simple is $49 a month plus $6 per person in one state, Plus is $80 plus $12 and covers several states, and Premium is $180 plus $22 with HR guidance. Gusto can also arrange health benefits for you.

Best for: small practices that want simple payroll and HR and keep their own employer accounts. Limits: you remain the only employer, so HR compliance stays with you.

BambooHR

BambooHR is HR software first. A November 9, 2026 check of its pricing page shows flat fees for 25 or fewer employees: Core $250, Pro $425 and Elite $650 a month. Above 25 staff it charges per employee. Payroll and benefits administration are add-ons.

Best for: practices that want strong employee records, onboarding and reporting. Limits: payroll costs extra, so total it before you compare.

Paycor

Paycor is now part of Paychex. Its small business page lists Basic at $99 a month plus $5 per employee, Essential at $149 plus $7 and Core at $199 plus $8, with setup fees by plan. Paycor also has a page for healthcare employers.

Best for: groups that want payroll, time and HR tools in one place. Limits: confirm current prices and setup fees in your quote.

How to choose

  1. Under 10 staff, one state, no benefits yet: Gusto or Justworks Payroll, and add a PEO only when you want group health.
  2. 10 to 50 staff, no HR lead, health quotes high: get quotes from Justworks, TriNet, Insperity and one of ADP TotalSource or Paychex.
  3. A broker you trust and an office manager who owns HR: BambooHR, Gusto, Paycor or Rippling.
  4. Several locations, states or entities: Rippling, Paycor or a large PEO. Ask each to show reporting by location.
  5. Remote billing staff outside the U.S.: look at an employer of record such as Deel, separate from your U.S. setup.

Ask every vendor five questions. What is my total monthly cost at my headcount, with premiums? What happens to our health plan if we leave mid-year? Is the PEO certified by the IRS as a CPEO? Can we track license expiry dates with alerts? Can pay be tagged by location and provider for our books?

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Should a 25-person practice use a PEO?

Often yes, if you want large-group health benefits and have no one to run HR. Price at least three PEOs and compare them with HR software plus a broker. Use the total cost, fees plus premiums, not the fee alone.

What does a PEO cost per employee?

The U.S. Chamber of Commerce puts it at $40 to $200 per employee per month, or 2 to 12 percent of gross payroll. Justworks lists $79 and $124 per employee. Most other PEOs quote.

Is Justworks, Rippling or TriNet better for a medical practice?

Justworks suits small practices that want a posted price. Rippling suits groups that want software that can grow beyond a PEO. TriNet suits growing groups that want a large PEO. Get a quote from each at your headcount.

What is a CPEO?

A CPEO is a PEO the IRS has certified under section 7705 of the tax code. The IRS publishes a list of certified PEOs. Ask any PEO whether it is certified and how it handles federal employment taxes.

Does HR software track nurse and clinician licenses?

Most HR platforms let you add license fields and expiry dates to each employee record. Ask the vendor to show alerts before expiry. Payer credentialing is a separate process.

Can I leave a PEO later?

Yes, but plan the timing. Leaving mid-year can mean moving staff to a new health plan and new payroll accounts. Ask about exit terms before you sign the paperwork.
