What practice owners ask before switching

How fast can my practice get started?

Most practices are live in about 24 hours. We handle the switch from your current bookkeeper or CPA, clean up prior months, and set everything up - with zero disruption to patient care.

Is my practice data secure and HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. Lucid uses bank-level encryption and SOC 2 and HIPAA-aligned controls. Your data is never sold and never used to train outside AI models. See our Security & Trust Center for details.

Do I work with a real person, or just AI?

Both. AI keeps your books current in real time, and you get a dedicated CPA who knows your specialty and owns your numbers. It's a high-touch service with a real expert on your team, available 24/7.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is transparent and starts at $130/mo, scaling with your practice's size. Use the calculator above for an instant estimate - no demo required, no hidden fees, no surprise setup charge.

What if you don't find any tax credits?

Then you pay $0 for credits work. We only get paid for credits if you actually receive them - so there is no risk in letting us look.

Can you handle multiple locations or entities?

Yes. Lucid supports solo practices through multi-location groups and MSOs, with consolidated reporting, multi-state filings, and benchmarking across every entity.
