Why it matters

The end of end-of-month cleanup

For most practices, categorization is a monthly scramble: a bookkeeper guesses at hundreds of transactions weeks after they happened, and errors quietly compound until tax season. That lag is where inaccurate books, missed deductions, and painful closes come from.

Lucid's Categorization platform classifies every transaction the instant it posts, using an AI engine trained on how medical and private practices actually spend and earn - payer deposits, private pay, clinical supplies, equipment, and specialty-specific costs. Every decision is explainable, so your books are accurate, audit-ready, and trusted from day one.
