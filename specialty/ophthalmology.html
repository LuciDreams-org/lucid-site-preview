The real problems

Money problems we see in eye care practices

Optical and exam income land in one account

The exam side sells services and the optical sells frames and lenses. In one income account, you cannot see optical margin or exam margin.

How Lucid handles it: Lucid rebuilds your chart of accounts so exam, medical, surgical and optical revenue each have their own lines. Your monthly report shows optical and exam margin side by side.

Vision plans and medical insurance pay on different clocks

Vision plans and medical carriers pay on different rules and timing. One blended receivables number can hide a slow medical side.

How Lucid handles it: Lucid does not file claims or chase insurance. Lucid books each income type separately, reconciles deposits and tracks payer mix, so you can take specifics to your biller.

Profit on paper, shrinking cash

Equipment loan principal, owner draws and tax payments are not expenses on the P&L, yet they take cash.

How Lucid handles it: Lucid shows profit and cash side by side and models payback before you buy an OCT or a laser. Your CPA walks through buy, lease and finance options and the tax effect.

Questions, answered

Questions eye care owners ask

How do I know if my optical is actually making money?

Your books need separate lines for exam income, optical income, and the cost of frames and lenses. Once those are split, you can see optical gross margin on its own, and by location if you have more than one. Lucid sets this up and reports it monthly so you are not guessing.

Why is cash tight when my vision plan claims pay fast?

Vision plans and medical insurance usually pay on different timelines, and one blended receivables number can hide slow medical claims. Lucid separates the two in your books and tracks payer mix, so you can see which side is slowing down. Your biller handles the claims themselves.

How should I book contact lens rebates?

Rebates generally reduce the cost of the lenses you bought rather than count as regular sales. Your CPA confirms the treatment for your books so your optical margin stays accurate.

Do I charge sales tax on glasses and contact lenses?

It depends on your state, and the rules differ for exams and for eyewear. Your CPA can tell you what applies in your state. Your state tax authority has the final word on what applies to you.

Does Lucid handle my billing or insurance claims?

No. Lucid works alongside your biller. Lucid handles the books, taxes and CFO reporting, and gives you clear numbers on margin by location, provider and payer mix.
