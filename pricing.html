FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How does Lucid pricing work?

One transparent monthly price that scales with your practice's size, starting at $130/mo. Slide the calculator to your spend or revenue for an instant estimate. Choose yearly billing to get two months free.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

No. There's no setup fee and no surprise onboarding charge - the price you see is the price you pay.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute consult with a Lucid expert. We'll learn about your practice, show you exactly what's included, and get you set up - most practices are live in about 7 days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Bookkeeping is month-to-month with no annual lock-in. Yearly billing is optional and simply adds two months free.

What about tax credits?

Our experts find every credit your practice qualifies for - an average of $50,711 - and handle the paperwork. We only get paid if you do; if no credits can be claimed, you pay $0.

Is my data secure and HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. Lucid uses bank-level encryption and is aligned with SOC 2 and HIPAA controls. Your data is never sold or used to train outside AI models. See our Security & Trust Center.
