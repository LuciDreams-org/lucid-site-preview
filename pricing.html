FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What's included in the Lucid Plan?

Dedicated bookkeeping experts, accurate monthly books, a year-end tax-ready financial package, and P&L and balance sheet reporting - all structured for how medical and private practices actually earn.

How does the All-in-One Plan differ?

It adds unlimited communication with your team, scenario planning, real-time insights, and full tax filings on top of bookkeeping. For pricing details tailored to your practice, talk to an expert.

How does the pricing calculator work?

Your monthly price scales with your practice's spending volume. Slide to your typical monthly spend (or revenue) and you'll see a clear estimate. Choose yearly billing to get two months free.

What about tax credits?

Our experts find every credit your practice qualifies for and handle the paperwork. We only get paid if you do - if no credits can be claimed, you pay $0.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Lucid is built with enterprise-grade security and is fully aligned with SOC 2 compliance standards.
