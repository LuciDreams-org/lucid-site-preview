SaaS Financial Benchmarks 2024

December 21, 2024 · 7 min read

SaaS companies in 2024 are shifting focus from rapid growth to efficient revenue growth. This comes after a challenging 2023, where revenue growth efficiency declined. To stay competitive, tracking key metrics like Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and Customer Churn Rate is essential. AI-powered tools like Lucid Financials, Benchmarkit, and Maxio provide real-time insights and benchmarks to help SaaS businesses improve financial planning and performance.

Key Takeaways:

  • Lucid Financials: AI-driven financial planning with scenario modeling and integrations like QuickBooks.
  • Benchmarkit: Focuses on CAC efficiency and peer comparisons using data from 1,000+ B2B SaaS companies.
  • Maxio: Offers industry-specific benchmarks and custom peer grouping for established SaaS businesses.

FAQs

What is the average ARR multiple for SaaS?

In 2024, the average Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) multiple for SaaS companies stands at 5.5x - the lowest it’s been in three years. For instance, a company with $10M in ARR would have a valuation of approximately $55M. This reflects a shift in focus toward efficiency and steady growth after the challenges of 2023.

SaaS companies can use this figure to shape their approach to:

  • Fundraising efforts
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Evaluating market position

Financial planning platforms are key to navigating these valuations effectively:
