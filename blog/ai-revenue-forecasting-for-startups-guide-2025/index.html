Preparing Data for AI Analysis

Structured and clean data is key to accurate AI forecasting. Poorly formatted data can lead to errors and unreliable forecasts.

Step What to Do
Format Conversion Convert financial statements into CSV format.
Data Cleaning Remove merged cells, logos, and unnecessary formatting.
Standardization Use consistent column headers and clear, descriptive labels.
Number Formatting Ensure all numerical data is stored as numbers, not as text.

Consistency across time periods and placeholders for missing values can help AI models identify patterns more effectively, leading to better forecasts.

Predictive analytics uses historical data and market trends to form the backbone of AI-driven revenue forecasting. Machine Learning Applications

Machine learning takes predictive analytics to the next level by refining forecasts over time. As it processes more data, it improves accuracy without requiring manual adjustments.
