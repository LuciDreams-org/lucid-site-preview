Who does my practice have to send a 1099 to for 2026?

December 4, 2026 · 9 min read

By Lucid · Last reviewed December 4, 2026

For payments you make in 2026, you send Form 1099‑NEC to each contractor you paid $2,000 or more for services, unless the contractor is a corporation. Attorneys get one even as a corporation, and corporations that provide health care get a 1099‑MISC. You skip payments made by card or PayPal. You send 1099‑NECs by February 1, 2027.

Key points

  • The 1099 threshold for payments you make in 2026 is $2,000 per vendor for the year, up from $600.
  • An LLC gets a 1099 unless its Form W‑9 says it's taxed as a C or S corporation.
  • A professional corporation you pay for health care services gets Form 1099‑MISC, box 6.
  • Form 1099‑NEC is due Monday, February 1, 2027, because January 31 is a Sunday.

What do the 1099 forms and terms mean?

Form 1099‑NEC is the form for $2,000 or more of pay for services to someone who isn't your employee. Form 1099‑MISC is the form for rent, health care payments and some other payments. Form W‑9 is the form a vendor fills in to give you its legal name, tax classification and taxpayer identification number (TIN). Form 1099‑K is the form card companies and payment apps file for the payments they processed. Backup withholding is the 24% you hold back from a vendor's payments when it doesn't give you a TIN.

What is the 1099 threshold for 2026 payments?

For payments you make in 2026, the threshold is $2,000 per vendor for the year, up from $600 for 2025. You add up what you paid that vendor by check, ACH or cash, and you send a form once the total reaches $2,000. The same $2,000 applies to attorneys' fees, rent and health care payments.

Congress raised the threshold in the July 2025 tax law (P.L. 119-21), and the IRS may adjust it for inflation starting in 2027.

Which vendors at a medical practice get a 1099?

Most vendors you paid $2,000 or more by check or bank transfer get one, unless they're a corporation. Corporations usually don't, with two exceptions: attorneys, and providers of medical or health care services. Your employees get a W‑2, and you send nothing for payments you made by card or payment app.

For a professional corporation's patient care, you report the whole payment, including injections or drugs, in box 6 under the corporation's name. You skip pharmacies, tax‑exempt hospitals and merchandise, such as injectables from a distributor.

Do I send a 1099 to an LLC or a corporation?

You send one to an LLC unless it's taxed as a C or S corporation. Check line 3a of its Form W‑9: an LLC that wrote C or S counts as a corporation, and one that wrote P gets a 1099.

A disregarded entity is a single‑member LLC that the IRS taxes as part of its owner. Its owner checks the owner's own box on line 3a, usually "Individual/sole proprietor", and you put the owner's name and number on the 1099.

A practice that paid the nine vendors below in 2026 would send six forms: four 1099‑NECs and two 1099‑MISCs. Three vendors get nothing from the practice: a corporation, a vendor the practice paid by card, and one it paid less than $2,000.

Do I send a 1099 to a contractor I paid by card or PayPal?

Not for the part you paid by credit card, debit card or a payment app such as PayPal for goods and services. The card company or app reports those payments on Form 1099‑K, so you leave them off your 1099‑NEC.

You leave them off even if the contractor never gets a 1099‑K. Payment apps must send one only above $20,000 and 200 transactions a year.

When are 1099s due in 2027?

Form 1099‑NEC is due to your contractors and to the IRS by Monday, February 1, 2027, because January 31 falls on a Sunday. You send recipients their Form 1099‑MISC copies by February 1 too, but you have until March 1 on paper, or March 31 electronically, to file the IRS copy.

Date What you do Form
Early January 2027 Run 2026 payments by vendor; ask for missing W‑9s Your own step
Mon, Feb 1, 2027 Send to contractors and file with the IRS 1099‑NEC
Mon, Feb 1, 2027 Send copies to recipients 1099‑MISC
Mon, Mar 1, 2027 File the IRS copy on paper 1099‑MISC
Wed, Mar 31, 2027 File the IRS copy electronically 1099‑MISC

Form 8809 gets you an automatic 30‑day extension for the IRS copy of a 1099‑MISC, but not for a 1099‑NEC. For the rest of next year's federal dates, see Lucid's 2027 tax deadlines guide for medical practices.

How do I collect W‑9s from vendors?

Ask each vendor for a Form W‑9 before you pay the first invoice, not in January. You don't send the W‑9 to the IRS; you keep it and use it to fill in the 1099. If a vendor doesn't give you a TIN, you must withhold 24% from its later payments and send that to the IRS.

A W‑9 checklist for your office manager:

  1. Ask for the W‑9 with the vendor's first invoice.
  2. Check that line 1 shows the legal name and line 3a has one box checked, plus the letter C, S or P for an LLC.
  3. Check that the TIN has nine digits: an SSN for a person, an EIN for a business.
  4. Save it with the vendor in your books, and keep it private.
  5. Ask for a new one when a vendor changes its name, entity or tax status.

If the IRS writes to say a vendor's name and number don't match, read what to do with a B notice.

What happens if I miss a 1099 or file it late?

The IRS can charge your practice a penalty for each form you file late, file wrong or don't file, and a second one for each copy you don't give the vendor. In the IRS's current instructions, the filing penalty starts at $60 a form within 30 days and reaches $340 a form after August 1.

Practices averaging $5 million or less in gross receipts have lower yearly caps, and the IRS can waive the penalty for reasonable cause.

What should med spa and medical group owners check?

Med spa owners usually pay their largest 1099 amounts to a medical director and contract injectors. Medical group owners file 1099s from each business that paid the vendor. Both should confirm before January that every contractor is really a contractor, because an employee gets a W‑2.

Questions practice owners ask about 1099s

This guide is general information, not tax advice for your practice. Tax rules change and depend on your state and your numbers. Talk to a CPA before you act on it.

Does my medical director get a 1099?

Usually yes, if you paid your medical director $2,000 or more in 2026 outside payroll. A director you pay as an individual or through an LLC gets a 1099‑NEC. One you pay through a professional corporation gets a 1099‑MISC, box 6, if the fee is for medical services. A director on your payroll gets a W‑2 instead.

Do I send a 1099 to an LLC or a corporation?

To an LLC, usually yes; to a corporation, usually no. An LLC that wrote C or S on its W‑9 counts as a corporation. If an LLC tells you mid‑year that it elected S corporation status, ask it for a new W‑9 before you file. Corporations still get a form for legal fees and health care services.

What is the 1099 threshold for 2026 payments?

It's $2,000 per vendor for everything you paid them in 2026. It was $600 for payments through 2025, so a vendor you paid $1,500 last year got a form and won't this year. One amount stayed at $600 in the IRS instructions: settlement money you pay to an attorney, reported in 1099‑MISC, box 10.

When are 1099s due in 2027?

You send Form 1099‑NEC to contractors and the IRS by Monday, February 1, 2027. If your practice files 10 or more information returns in total, counting W‑2s, you must file them electronically. You can file your 1099s through the IRS's IRIS portal. A practice with six 1099s and four W‑2s is already at 10.

Do I send a 1099 to a contractor I paid by card or PayPal?

Not for those payments. The card company or payment app reports them on Form 1099‑K, so you leave them off your 1099‑NEC. This applies to PayPal payments for goods and services, not personal transfers. If you also paid the contractor by check or ACH, you report those payments once they reach $2,000.

What happens if I miss a 1099?

File it as soon as you notice, because the penalty per form grows the longer you wait: $60 within 30 days and $130 by August 1 in the current IRS instructions. Send the vendor a copy too, since a missing vendor copy carries its own penalty.
