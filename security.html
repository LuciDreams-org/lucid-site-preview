How we protect your data

Six layers of protection, by default

🔒

Encryption & access

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Access is least-privilege, logged, and protected with multi-factor authentication.

🛡️

SOC 2 controls

Our security program is aligned to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria - security, availability, and confidentiality - with continuous monitoring and annual review.

🏥

HIPAA & BAAs

HIPAA-aligned handling for practice data, and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is available for practices that require one. Ask your expert to start one.

🤖

Responsible AI

AI keeps your books current, and your dedicated CPA (available 24/7) owns the final numbers. Your data is never sold and never used to train third-party AI models.

🏦

Read-only bank links

Bank and card connections are read-only through trusted aggregators. Lucid cannot move your money - we only see the transactions needed to keep your books.

✅

Accuracy guarantee

We stand behind our work. If we make a bookkeeping error, we fix it and cover any resulting penalty - and if we find no tax credits, you pay $0.
