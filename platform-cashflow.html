Why it matters

Stop running your practice on last month's numbers

Most practices only see their financial picture weeks after the month closes - long after the moment a decision needed to be made. By then, the overtime was already paid, the slow season already bit, and the new hire was already on payroll. Lucid changes the timing: your cash position, revenue, and spend are current to the moment money moves.

Medical and private practices earn in ways generic tools never model well - insurance lag, patient responsibility, payer mix, seasonality, and multi-location overhead. The Cashflow platform is built around those realities, so what you see on screen actually reflects how your practice runs, and what you forecast is something you can act on with confidence.
