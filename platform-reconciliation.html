Why it matters

Books that are always closed, not closed once a month

Traditional reconciliation happens once a month, in a rush, and anything that does not match becomes a time-consuming hunt. Until it is done, you are making decisions on numbers that might move. For a busy practice, that is a real risk hiding in plain sight.

Lucid reconciles continuously. Bank, card, and merchant feeds are matched automatically as they arrive, and the AI surfaces only the exceptions that genuinely need a human - the small percentage that matters, not the mountain that does not. The result is books that are always closed, always accurate, and always ready for your CPA or an audit.

How it works

Continuous matching, exception-first review

1

Feeds arrive and match automatically

Every bank, card, and merchant transaction is matched against your books as it comes in - continuously, not monthly.

2

AI surfaces only the exceptions

The engine clears the routine matches and flags the small share that needs a human decision, with context attached.

3

Your team resolves and moves on

You and your dedicated CPA handle the exceptions quickly, and your books stay closed and audit-ready at all times.
